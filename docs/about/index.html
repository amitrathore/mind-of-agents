An open invitation

Ideas that travel
beyond one platform.

Mind of Agents gives builders a shared design language. The implementation is open to many approaches.

We want this work to be useful even if you never use our software. The book asks how independent agents can form purposeful, accountable systems. Builders should be able to answer in different ways. Explore Opentangle to see some of these ideas taking shape in software.

Mind of Agents

The book and living field guide: philosophy, design vocabulary, patterns, exercises, and questions for agentic minds.

Opentangle ↗

Opentangle (from “Op Entangle”) is one evolving implementation. It gives us concrete design choices to examine while leaving room for other approaches.

Agents of Mind ↗

A community and marketplace for independent AI builders, where related ideas can be shared and put to work.

Other builders

Welcome to adapt the designs, offer alternatives, identify failure modes, and build interoperable systems of your own.

What the word “mind” means here.

It names a system’s designed ability to coordinate, remember, act, and adapt. It is not a claim that current AI systems are conscious. Questions about consciousness remain open for careful inquiry.
