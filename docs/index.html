A field guide to agentic minds: human and AI agents that remember, coordinate, act, and learn together while making their contributions visible in an independent, interconnected knowledge economy.

Independent by designInterconnected by choiceOpen to many implementations
The central idea

An agent is a start.
A mind is a design.

More agents do not automatically create collective intelligence. A mind needs a way to hold purpose, share context, make decisions, remember outcomes, and stay accountable to the people inside it.

Explore the anatomy of an agentic mind ↗

01 / AGENCY

Independent actors.

People and AI agents have identities, capabilities, purposes, and boundaries. Their independence is a design requirement.

02 / CONNECTION

Shared ways to work.

Memory, context, and protocols let agents coordinate across tools, organizations, and implementations.

03 / LEARNING

Outcomes that matter.

Actions leave an inspectable trail. People can learn what worked, recognize contributions, and decide how the value of shared work should be distributed.

The design question

What can we do together?

Begin with a shared purpose. Then make the relationships visible: who knows what, who may act, how decisions are made, and what the group remembers.
