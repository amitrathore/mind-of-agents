An invitation to build

Start with the work. Then design the mind.

Tell me what you’re trying to make possible. We can explore the philosophy and architecture, shape a first prototype, or work through implementation together.

This invitation is for independent builders, teams, and businesses. Your approach does not have to use Opentangle; the ideas in Mind of Agents are meant to travel.

A useful first conversation might ask:
  • What should this mind notice and remember?
  • Where should agents act independently, and where should people decide?
  • How will its parts coordinate and remain accountable?
